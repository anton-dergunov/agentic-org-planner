> One of Vienna’s most magical hidden gems ✨

> This is the Ernst Fuchs Museum, @ernstfuchsmuseum located inside Otto Wagner’s Villa I — a former home designed by Otto Wagner himself, one of the most influential Austrian architects and a key figure of Viennese Modernism.
>
> Later, the villa was bought by Austrian artist Ernst Fuchs, who transformed it into this surreal, colorful and almost mystical place. Today you can explore the villa, its interiors, paintings, sculptures and incredible mosaics.
>
